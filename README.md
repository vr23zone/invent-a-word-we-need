**pocketphantom — the buzz you feel in your pocket from a phone that isn't there.**

Phantom vibration is a real, documented feeling almost every phone owner knows — yet English has no single word for it. *Pocketphantom* fills that gap: sayable, spellable, and instantly understood the first time you hear it.
